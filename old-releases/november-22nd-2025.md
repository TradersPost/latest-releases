# November 22nd, 2025

## Combined Entry and Exit Settings

All entry-related settings are now grouped together in a single Entry section, and all exit-related settings are grouped in the Exit section. This makes strategy setup more straightforward and reduces the need to jump around the page.\
\
Take-profit and stop-loss settings were also moved into the Entry section since they are sent as additional legs on the entry order.

## Live Search Forms

All search forms now update results instantly as you type, with no need to hit enter or reload the page. Your search filters are also remembered across pages, so when you come back to a list, your filters are still applied and you can pick up right where you left off.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-11-24 at 4.54.17 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Forms Redesigned

We redesigned all forms to make them easier to use. Many select dropdowns were replaced with clean button groups, which makes the options easier to see and faster to tap through. The goal was to make the forms feel more intuitive and reduce the friction when setting up a strategy or configuring an order.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-11-24 at 4.54.17 PM (1).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## CSV Exports

You can now export your data to CSV with a single click, making it easy to download and analyze your trades in your own tools.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2025-11-24 at 4.58.10 PM.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Select List Items

You can now select items in any list. When you select one or more items, a bar appears at the bottom of the page with actions you can run on the selected items, making bulk updates faster and easier.

## Moved Subscription Defaults

Subscription defaults have been moved to the bottom of the strategy edit screen for easier access. They now open in a modal instead of a separate page, so you can review and update your defaults without navigating away from your strategy.
