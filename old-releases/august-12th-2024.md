# August 12th, 2024

## Tastytrade Beta

We made the [Tastytrade](https://traderspost.io/broker/tastytrade) integration available for all TradersPost customers. Please remember that this integration is in <mark style="color:orange;">**BETA**</mark> and you may experience issues that we have not discovered yet. Read more about this integration in the [Tastytrade Documentation](https://docs.traderspost.io/docs/core-concepts/brokers/broker-roadmap/tastytrade). If you have any issues or questions, please email us at [support@traderspost.io](mailto:support@traderspost.io)

## Robinhood Retirement Accounts

TradersPost now supports selecting between multiple accounts when connecting to Robinhood. If you have retirement accounts linked to Robinhood, they will also be visible when connecting Robinhood to TradersPost.

## Handle No Broker Accounts

When you connect a broker to TradersPost, the connection might be successful, but no accounts may be returned, and it might not be obvious that something is wrong. This fix adds a clear error message indicating that no accounts were returned by the broker. It advises contacting the broker's support team to resolve the issue.
