# November 9th, 2024

## tastytrade Improvements

* tastytrade now supports the options asset class in addition to stocks, futures and crypto.
* You can now connect your tastytrade account to TradersPost through OAuth. If you've already connected your account to TradersPost, it will continue to work. The next time you connect yout tastytrade account, it will connect through OAuth.
* We now support the ability fetch quotes from tastytrade for all asset classes.
