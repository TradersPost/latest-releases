# August 20th, 2025



## TradeStation Free Options Trading

TradersPost now provides free options trading exclusively through TradeStation. Previously, there was a $0.60 fee per contract, but now it's completely free! For more information on the TradeStation integration, click [here](https://traderspost.io/broker/tradestation).

## **TradersPost Affiliate Program Update**

The TradersPost affiliate program now includes support for affiliate coupon codes. Affiliates can create multiple coupon codes for various campaigns, allowing for better tracking of leads and conversions through the affiliate dashboard. Additionally, the affiliate dashboard has been revamped to help manage all program-related information more efficiently.
