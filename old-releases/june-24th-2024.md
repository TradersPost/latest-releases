# June 24th, 2024

## Improve Use Signal Toggles

Checking or unchecking the master **Use signal settings** toggle will now toggle all the other use signal toggles without having to save the form first. Similarly, if you have **Use signal settings** checked and you uncheck one of the lower level settings, it will uncheck the master setting without having to save first.

## Discord Warning

When clicking to join a Discord invite, we have added an important warning. Be aware of scammers, phishers, and other malicious actors who may attempt to direct message (DM) you and impersonate TradersPost. These individuals might try to deceive you into sharing sensitive information or performing actions that could compromise your security. Always verify the identity of anyone contacting you and report any suspicious activity immediately. Stay vigilant and protect your personal information at all times.

## Fix Error Connecting Broker to Strategy

We fixed an 500 internal server error that would occur when you try to connect a broker to a strategy that does not support the strategies asset class. For example, if you tried to connect Alpaca to a futures strategy, you would get an internal server error.

## Show Logs From Broker Dashboard

You can now quickly get to the broker logs from the broker dashboard by clicking the 3 dots button and then clicking Logs.
