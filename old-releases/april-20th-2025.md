# April 20th, 2025

## Expanded Broker Support with Add-Ons

In this release, we introduced the ability for users to purchase additional broker connections beyond what is included in their plan. Users can now add:

* **Additional Paper Brokers** for **$5 per month** each
* **Additional Live Brokers** for **$10 per month** each

This feature gives users greater flexibility to manage multiple trading accounts across different brokers, whether for testing (paper) or real trading (live). You can easily manage and purchase additional broker add-ons directly from your billing settings.

If you reach your included broker limit and want to connect another account, you’ll now be prompted with the option to add more — making it simple to scale your trading setup as needed.
