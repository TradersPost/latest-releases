# June 16th, 2025

**New: Hide Private Content with a Click**

We've added a new eye icon button in the TradersPost sidebar that lets you quickly hide private content on the page. This is perfect for taking clean screenshots without exposing sensitive information like account names, broker connections, or plan details. Just click the eye icon to toggle between visible and hidden content.
